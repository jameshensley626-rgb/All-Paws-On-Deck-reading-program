Our mission

A friend for every reader.

We aim to help children discover a lifelong love of reading through positive, supervised experiences with trained therapy dogs. Every student deserves encouragement, regardless of their reading level or learning needs.

Our literacy program

How a reading session works

A simple, school-supervised routine designed to complement existing literacy instruction, not replace it.

Choose a book

A teacher or reading specialist helps the student select a book suited to their interests and reading needs.

Read with a paw partner

The student reads aloud with an approved therapy dog and its adult handler nearby, with school staff supervision.

Celebrate progress

Staff encourage the student's effort, discuss the story when appropriate and monitor progress using school-approved tools.

Our canine program

Good listeners come with four paws.

The program would partner only with school-approved therapy dog and handler teams. No dog is expected to teach reading; their role is to provide calm companionship.

  • Dogs evaluated for suitability around children and in school settings.
  • Adult handlers trained by an appropriate therapy animal organization.
  • Current health records and any required insurance or school clearances.
  • Rest breaks, water, and the ability for dogs to leave a session when needed.
Safety comes first

Safe for every student. Safe for every dog.

  • Parent or guardian permission before participation.
  • School staff and approved adult handlers present.
  • Hygiene procedures and school-approved animal contact rules.
  • Accommodations for allergies, fears, disabilities and students who prefer a dog-free option.
  • All participation is voluntary.

Final requirements will be established with each participating school and therapy dog organization.

For families

Questions from parents

What families should know about the proposed program.

Does my child have to be a strong reader?

No. The program is designed to welcome students at different reading levels. The school would help select appropriate books and support each student's learning needs.

What if my child is allergic to or afraid of dogs?

Participation would be optional. Schools would plan accommodations and a dog-free reading option so students can receive literacy support without animal contact.

Who supervises the sessions?

The proposed model requires school staff supervision and an approved adult therapy dog handler. Dogs and children would not be left alone together.

Can families sign up now?

Not yet. All Paws on Deck is in development. Enrollment would open only through participating schools after necessary approvals and safety arrangements are in place.

Does the website collect student information?

No. This website does not contain enrollment forms, tracking scripts, or student accounts. Any future enrollment and records would be handled through school-approved processes.
