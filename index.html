Why we exist

Every reader deserves encouragement.

Our mission is to inspire a lifelong love of reading through positive, supervised experiences with dogs. We aim to complement school literacy instruction while helping children feel more comfortable practicing aloud.

Meet our canine staff

Our four-legged team.

Founding canine team member

Carolina

Golden Retriever  ·  8 years old

Carolina is the first and currently only member of our canine staff. She is a sweet, people-loving golden retriever who represents the friendship and kindness at the heart of All Paws on Deck. Her love of spending time with people makes her a wonderful ambassador for our motto, “All Paws Deserve Pals.”

Carolina is not represented as a certified therapy dog. Participation in school reading sessions would require appropriate evaluation, handler arrangements and school approval.

We look forward to growing our canine team as approved dog-and-handler partnerships become available.

Student & animal welfare

Safety comes before every session.

Our proposed model requires qualified, school-approved therapy dog teams. Dogs are companions, not teachers or substitutes for professional literacy instruction.

  • Appropriate canine evaluation, health records and required insurance.
  • Approved adult handlers and school staff present throughout sessions.
  • Regular rest, water and the option for dogs to leave.

Every child is welcome.

  • Voluntary participation and parent or guardian permission.
  • Hygiene and safe animal-contact procedures.
  • Accommodations for allergies, fear of dogs and disabilities.
  • A dog-free literacy option for students who prefer it.

Final requirements are subject to school and therapy organization approval.

For families

Frequently asked questions.

Does my child need to be a strong reader?

No. The proposed program welcomes students at different reading levels, with support from school staff.

What if my child has allergies or is afraid of dogs?

Participation would be voluntary, with appropriate accommodations and a dog-free alternative.

Who supervises reading sessions?

School staff and an approved adult handler would supervise. Children and dogs would never be left alone together.

Can Carolina visit my school now?

Not through this program yet. Any participating dog must satisfy school and therapy dog requirements before attending sessions.

How can my child join?

Enrollment is not open. Participating schools would manage enrollment and parent or guardian consent after approval.

Does the website collect student information?

No. This informational website has no enrollment forms, student accounts or tracking scripts. Future student records would be handled only through school-approved processes.
